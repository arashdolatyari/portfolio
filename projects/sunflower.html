The product

Sunflower is an app that helps people stay sober from any substance. For every sober day, people get a sunflower, so they can see their progress and feel rewarded. The app also has CBT-based journaling for cravings, an AI sponsor people can talk to at any time, learning content, and a community of people in recovery.

Sunflower is backed by Y Combinator (Fall 2025 batch) and a16z. It grew from 200 to 100,000 monthly active users in less than six months, and it has more than half a million users.

My role

I worked on Sunflower as a UI/UX designer through Synthetic Solutions, a product design agency. I designed the experience of new features and flows, and then I designed the UI for each flow.

People who use Sunflower are in recovery, and many of them are going through a hard time. Every flow had to feel encouraging, not clinical, and every paywall had to respect this.

Features and flows I designed

  • OnboardingThe first steps new members take in the app.
  • Breathing exercisesGuided exercises that help people get through a craving.
  • Progress statsA clear view of each person's sober time and milestones.
  • CommunityA place to share and support others in recovery.
  • Friend challengesChallenges people do together with their friends.
  • Paywalls and subscriptionsSubscription screens designed with care for a sensitive audience.
  • Letter to my future selfA letter people write to themselves and open one year later.
  • Live support groupsFree group sessions on Zoom, led by a licensed counselor.

Design system and visual identity

Besides the flows, I built the app's design system and worked on its visual identity:

  • Logos
  • An icon pack for each type of addiction
  • Home-screen widgets
  • Milestone templates people can share when they reach a sober milestone
